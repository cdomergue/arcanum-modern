## Highlights

- **Floating damage and healing:** health lost appears in red above characters and creatures; health restored appears in green. The numbers rise and fade away, making attacks, spells and healing easier to read at a glance.
- **Improved pathfinding:** click farther away while zoomed out and let your character find a route around obstacles. Local-map journeys use the same improved navigation, with fewer automatic intermediate points. Clicking a chest or another interactive object now seeks a reachable place to use it, including during combat.

## Other features

- **Explore with a companion:** move a controlled companion away from the hero without being teleported back. Release control to let them rejoin the party as usual.
- **Free camera:** the view keeps the controlled character in sight and advances farther ahead when they reach the edge, without a smooth sliding transition.
- **Offline save editor:** an optional [English-language browser tool](https://github.com/cdomergue/arcanum-modern/tree/v2.2.0/tools/save-editor) lets you edit your character's name, race, attributes, skills, spells and resources. It works locally and exports edited copies of your save files. Back up your saves before using it.

## Fixes

- Deploying a mechanized arachnid through a controlled companion's quick slot no longer causes runaway memory use and a game crash. The arachnid follows the companion who deployed it.
- In Follow Camera mode, movement keys move the selected companion correctly.
- Closing a dialogue while controlling a companion keeps the view on that companion. When releasing a distant companion's control, the free camera returns with them when they rejoin the hero.
- Local-map notes stay with their own town instead of appearing in other cities. Older personal notes whose town cannot be identified are preserved but hidden from local maps.
- Inventory sale feedback no longer overlaps the action icons.
- The Teleportation spell can target discovered, owned player homes, including the woodland house, subject to the usual spell restrictions.
- Fixed a Linux launcher startup failure.
- Fixed WorldED's empty object library on fresh installations.

Update through the Renaissance launcher, or install the package for your platform into your existing Arcanum folder. Your original game files are still required. Close the game before updating and back up your saves.
