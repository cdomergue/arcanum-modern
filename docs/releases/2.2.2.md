## Companions

- The journal has a new **Companions** chapter with each companion's latest recorded status, last known location and map coordinates.
- Existing saves can recover waiting companions when their saved relationship to your character is still present, including companions on other visited maps. Some older departures cannot be reconstructed.
- Companion records belong to the loaded save. Loading an earlier save restores its own journal state.
- Journal navigation fits longer French labels, and the companion information footer stays inside the frame.

## Maps

- Hover over the original game's **?** markers to read their descriptions in the modern interface.
- The map footer shows your current position and the coordinates under the cursor.
- Open **Note**, then choose **By click** to place a personal note or **By coordinates** to enter a precise location on the displayed map.
- The tutorial explains the Note menu and includes an updated English voice recording.

Update through the Renaissance launcher or install the package for your platform into your existing Arcanum folder. Close the game before updating and back up your saves.
