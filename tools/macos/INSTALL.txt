Arcanum Modern — macOS Apple Silicon (arm64)

Requires macOS 11 or later and your own Arcanum 1.0.7.4 game data.

1. Copy your installed game's files into a writable folder on your Mac.
2. Extract the ZIP there: Arcanum Modern.app and modern-ui/ must sit
   alongside arcanum1.dat, not inside the .app or in /Applications.
3. Open Arcanum Modern.app. Close the game before installing updates.

No Rosetta, Wine, Python or separate SDL installation is required to play.
The application is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. For a download
you trust, use the macOS Privacy & Security "Open Anyway" action if blocked.

Game data, saved games and personal preferences are not included. Existing
modern-ui settings and notebook files are retained when updating.
Bink cinematics are unavailable. This port needs gameplay validation on a Mac.

On first launch, HD local maps are generated from your installed game files.
A progress bar shows preparation. Allow about 8 GB plus temporary working space
for Arcanum and Vormantown. Choose "Play now" to skip preparation; it resumes
next time. Completed maps are reused. No extra downloads or tools are needed.
